# PROJECT AQRTI
## Intelligence Terminal Design

Version: 1.0

---

## PURPOSE

The dashboard is AQRTI's command center.

All intelligence must be visible from a single interface.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

- Dark Theme
- Institutional Feel
- High Information Density
- Fast Navigation
- No Clutter

## OBJECTIVE

Provide complete visibility into AQRTI intelligence and decision-making.
