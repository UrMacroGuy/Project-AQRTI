## Capital Preservation Framework

Version: 1.0

---

## PRIMARY RULE

- **Survive First.**
- **Profit Second.**

---

## PURPOSE

Prevent catastrophic losses.

A profitable system can recover.

A destroyed system cannot.

---

## RISK PYRAMID

```
     Market Risk
          ↓
    Strategy Risk
          ↓
    Position Risk
          ↓
   Execution Risk
          ↓
   Portfolio Risk
```

### Single Position
- **Initial:** 2% - 5%
- **Maximum:** 10%

### Sector Exposure
- **Maximum:** 25%

### Portfolio Exposure
- **Maximum:** 80%
- **Cash Reserve:** 20%

## POSITION SIZING

### Inputs:
- Confidence
- Risk Score
- Volatility
- Strategy Strength

### Output:
- Position Allocation

### CONFIDENCE ALLOCATION

- **90+ Confidence:** 5% Allocation
- **80-89 Confidence:** 4% Allocation
- **70-79 Confidence:** 3% Allocation
- **60-69 Confidence:** 1% Allocation
- **Below 60 Confidence:** No Trade

## REGIME PROTECTION

- **Bull Market:** Momentum Preferred
- **Bear Market:** Defensive Mode
