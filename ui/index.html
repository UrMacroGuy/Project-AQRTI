How the Arena Works
AUTO-PROMOTION GATE
Promoted algos with fitness ≥ 60 and win rate ≥ 52% are activated and enter paper quarantine — ≥60 days + ≥20 shadow trades required before real capital.
5-YEAR BACKTEST
Each algo is backtested over 5 years of real NSE price data with honest costs (0.28% round-trip), intrabar stop-loss, and no ML look-ahead.
PROMOTION GATES
All must pass: ≥60 backtest trades, Sharpe ≥0.5, WR ≥52%, OOS holdout, 0.8× NIFTY benchmark, duplicate check.
SELF-LEARNING EVOLUTION
Failing algos find donors (algos that profit on the loser's bad days). DSLs are merged — child must improve ≥50% of losing days while retaining ≥70% of winning days.
